token-optimize

One command that makes Claude Code, Claude chat and GitHub Copilot read less and answer shorter, in any project.

cd your-project
npx github:rahulcs754/token-optimize

The widget in the corner shows what the AI can read in a fresh Next.js app, with and without it. Hide it with the × button, or load your own report.

What it sets up

CLAUDE.mdShort brief with your real commands and token-saving rules.
Read-deny rulesClaude cannot open node_modules, build output, lockfiles or .env.
Copilot instructionsSame brief in .github/copilot-instructions.md.
Project mapA small folder map, so the AI skips exploring the repo.

See your own numbers

  1. In your project, run npx github:rahulcs754/token-optimize --audit --json > token-report.json
  2. Click Load my report in the widget and pick that file. It stays in your browser.

Put the widget on your site

<script src="https://rahulcs754.github.io/token-optimize/widget.js"
        data-report="/path/to/token-report.json"></script>

Visitors can hide it, and the choice is remembered in their browser. Your page can also call tokenOptimizeWidget.show() or .hide().

Save tokens in chat too

Most tokens go into the conversation itself. See the prompt habits for Claude Code, Claude chat and Copilot.
